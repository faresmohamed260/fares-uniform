## Scope and authority

This manifest records every file found in the connected KGC Google Drive folder at audit time. All 66 files are PNG images. The inventory is documentation evidence for public-site research and content planning; it does **not** approve publication, imply client permission, or authorize a deployment.

Fares confirmed the collection rules:

- every current KGC uniform asset is for the **National** program; there is no American Diploma content yet;
- the 20 worn/model images are the authoritative anchors: five each for Kindergarten, Primary, Middle and High;
- Kindergarten uses red academic polos; Primary uses white academic polos; Middle uses navy/light-blue/white diagonal polos; High uses navy/red/white diagonal polos;
- short-sleeve academic polos are summer looks and long-sleeve academic polos are winter looks;
- every stage has an academic summer look, academic winter look, hoodie look, puffer look and sports/training-suit look;
- the navy puffer and sports/training-suit designs are shared across all stages;
- the plain navy hoodie is shared by Kindergarten and Primary; Middle and High have their own hoodie designs;
- packshots count as current only when they visually match an authoritative worn/model anchor. Unmatched packshots remain explicitly extra and must not be forced into a stage or uniform set.

Exact file-to-look, garment and front/back assignments below are high-confidence visual inferences against those user-confirmed rules. Unverified commercial names, fabric specifications, sizes, production dates and publication rights are intentionally not invented.
