# Pattern in Motion — selected public-site design specification

**Status:** selected visual direction / kinetic design gate  
**Selected by:** Fares, 2026-09-18  
**Reference workflow:** GPT-6 Astra “10K Websites” process, adapted to Fares Uniform  
**Production status:** not approved for implementation or deployment

## 1. Selection receipt and boundary

Fares selected the second concept direction, **Pattern in Motion**, from three complete KGC-grounded visual directions. The selected concept is a bright editorial system built from the diagonal construction language already present in the KGC Middle and High polos. It is not a clone of any reference site and does not copy the Astra examples' brand, copy, palette or composition.

This document converts the selected visual direction into an implementation-independent specification. It authorizes the next kinetic prototype/review work only. It does not authorize production source changes, deployment, public publication of KGC media, provider mutation or Gate D closure.

The concept frames were reviewed in the design session but are not committed to this public repository because they contain KGC model imagery whose publication rights are not yet established. The canonical source references remain in [KGC National media manifest](KGC_MEDIA_MANIFEST.md); no KGC binary is copied into GitHub.

## 2. Creative thesis

**Designed as one. Worn for years.**

One continuous diagonal seam connects institutional identity, four educational stages, garment construction and enquiry. The page changes composition as the visitor moves through the story; it does not become a stack of unrelated sections or cards.

Emotional target:

- bright, calm and credible at rest;
- authored and memorable during product transitions;
- precise enough for a serious institutional buyer;
- recognizably about garments before long copy is read.

## 3. Visual system

| Element | Selected direction |
| --- | --- |
| Base surface | Warm chalk white with restrained textile grain |
| Primary ink | Deep navy |
| Signal colour | Oxblood/red |
| Secondary signal | Light blue |
| Structural motif | The real diagonal panel/seam language from KGC Middle and High polos |
| Display typography | High-contrast editorial serif with strong Arabic companion |
| Utility typography | Precise, highly legible sans with full Arabic coverage |
| Photography | Large clean model/product media; deliberate crops; garments remain truthful |
| Depth | Media, typography and diagonal textile planes overlap with explicit occlusion rules |

Final typefaces require licensing, Arabic coverage, performance and rendered pairing review. The concept commits to the relationship, not an unverified font product.

## 4. Information architecture

The selected full public journey is:

1. **Opening proposition** — institutional program capability and one clear action.
2. **Program continuity** — Kindergarten, Primary, Middle and High form one visual sequence.
3. **Garment selection** — one item isolates without losing the program context.
4. **Product inspection** — the garment moves into a truthful layered/exploded state.
5. **Reassembly** — the selected garment returns to the coordinated lineup.
6. **Process and capability** — understand, design, sample, manufacture and deliver.
7. **Contextual enquiry** — selected program, stage and garment remain visible in the enquiry handoff.

The public surface continues to expose no price and no stock. Enquiry, WhatsApp and phone remain the accepted conversion routes.

## 6. Layer contract

| Plane | Content | Movement | Contact / occlusion rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base | Chalk surface and minimal textile grain | Static | Must preserve legibility and fast first paint |
| Structural | Red, white and light-blue diagonal textile planes | Slow restrained displacement | May cross page edges and subject silhouette; never cross faces or required copy |
| Typography | Headline and large stage words | Small counter-movement | May sit behind the model but never lose critical words |
| Focal media | Selected model or garment | Deliberate continuity | Identity, colour and construction are not morphed |
| Detail media | Matched packshots and material crops | Enters only during inspection | Must trace to a manifest-approved anchor |
| Interface | Header, actions, labels, focus | Stable | Always readable, keyboard/touch accessible |

## 7. Interaction choreography

### Opening

- **Entry:** High-stage subject is already understandable before motion loads.
- **Movement:** the structural diagonal crosses the viewport while the subject gains controlled prominence; typography shifts less than the textile layer.
- **Settled state:** headline, subject and actions are simultaneously legible.
- **Reverse:** scrolling upward retraces the same geometry without popping or unrelated fades.
- **Interruption:** rapid scrolling resolves immediately to the nearest stable state.

### Four-stage rail

- Vertical progress may control a horizontal stage sequence only when the mapping is obvious.
- Stage labels remain available without hover.
- Pointer, keyboard, touch and scroll all reach the same selected state.
- Selecting a stage updates one continuous composition; it does not navigate through four disconnected cards.

### Exploded view

- Review route: **`/explodeview`** is the working name for the dedicated kinetic review surface. Production routing remains a later decision.
- The full garment is the origin and destination.
- Layers separate along meaningful construction boundaries visible in approved photography.
- Annotation focus may move the selected layer slightly and reveal a real crop; it must not claim hidden engineering truth.
- **Reassemble** reverses the same path.
- Front/back changes use approved packshots and preserve orientation.
- The enquiry action retains `program=KGC National`, `stage=High` and the selected garment context without exposing internal IDs.

## 8. Responsive art direction

### Desktop / pointer

- 1440px concept target; the subject and copy form an asymmetric split.
- The diagonal may travel across multiple viewport widths/heights.
- Hover can add bounded depth, but selection never depends on hover.
- Opening, midpoint and resolved exit must each form a complete composition.

### Mobile English

- Compose for 390px; do not squeeze desktop.
- Compact wordmark, language switch and menu remain stable.
- Headline, subject and first action are visible without an excessive cinematic wait.
- The stage rail becomes touch-first and may be horizontally scrollable with visible progress.
- Controls meet a practical 44×44px target.

### Mobile Arabic RTL

- Compose independently in RTL; do not mirror the final English screenshot mechanically.
- Copy aligns right, directional actions reverse meaningfully and the subject moves to preserve readable space.
- Approved opening copy:
  - **هوية واحدة. ترافقهم لسنوات.**
  - **برنامج زي موحّد لكل مرحلة، مصمّم بعناية ومُصنّع في مصر للمدارس والمؤسسات.**
  - **استكشف برنامج KGC**
  - **ناقش برنامجك معنا**
  - **برنامج واحد · أربع مراحل**
- Stage labels: **رياض الأطفال، الابتدائي، الإعدادي، الثانوي**.
- Mixed Latin names such as KGC remain isolated correctly.

## 9. Reduced motion and low-capability reading

When reduced motion is active:

- textile layers render directly in their settled positions;
- stage content becomes a normal vertical sequence;
- no scroll scrubbing, large shared travel, pointer-follow or pinned trap remains;
- exploded layers use a static ordered stack with the same annotations;
- reassembly becomes an immediate state change;
- all content, hierarchy and enquiry context remain complete.

If WebGL is unavailable, nothing is lost. This concept does not require real-time 3D.

## 10. Technology hypothesis

Technology remains subordinate to the accepted choreography:

- semantic Next.js/React content and controls;
- existing Motion direction for layout continuity, presence and component-level gestures;
- CSS clipping/masking only where it preserves real media;
- GSAP/ScrollTrigger only if the hosted kinetic proof shows timeline coordination cannot remain clear with the existing motion stack;
- no Lenis unless native scrolling fails an accepted choreography requirement;
- no Three.js/WebGL unless future trustworthy product geometry materially improves inspection;
- responsive images, lazy heavy media, instant header/actions and complete static fallbacks.

This is not dependency approval.

## 11. Media and truth rules

- The 20 worn/model images remain the current-look authority.
- Only the 36 matched/used packshots may accompany those anchors.
- The eight extra/unmatched packshots stay outside the current experience.
- KGC name, crest, model photos and building image remain publication-gated.
- No real KGC binary enters this public repository until approval is recorded.
- Generated concept media is illustrative and cannot become historical proof.
- A generated exploded arrangement is a visual inspection concept unless verified against the real garment/sample.

## 12. Kinetic prototype acceptance

The next reviewable prototype must demonstrate:

1. opening, midpoint and settled states for the hero;
2. reversible four-stage progression;
3. model/card-to-garment continuity;
4. exploded and reassembled High-uniform states;
5. annotation selection and front/back controls;
6. program/stage context carried into enquiry;
7. desktop pointer, mobile touch and keyboard operation;
8. English, representative Arabic RTL and realistic wrapping;
9. reduced-motion and low-capability fallbacks;
10. no price, stock, fabricated claims, public KGC binary, backend mutation or deployment.

Rendered temporal review is required. A successful build alone cannot pass the kinetic gate.
